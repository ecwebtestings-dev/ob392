# OB39 Ltd — Services Page: Content & Layout Guide

This document gives you ready-to-use copy for each section of the Services page, plus a suggested layout for each block so your designer/developer can build it directly.

---

## SECTION 3 — Business Incubation & Capital Pooling (Project L – Chapter A)

**Layout:** Two-column split — left: descriptive text + bullet list of services; right: supporting image or simple diagram of the "unit" cycle (60 members → daily pooled % → redistribution). Consider a small numbered-step strip beneath (1. Join a unit → 2. Contribute daily % → 3. Receive growth capital → 4. Expand your business) for visual clarity.

**Copy:**

**Turning Small Daily Contributions into Real Business Growth**

Many small businesses fail not from lack of ambition, but from lack of capital, structure, and support. OB39 Ltd organizes entrepreneurs into units of 60 businesses that pool a share of daily profits — 10%, 20%, or 30% — into a secure, tripartite-authorized fund. These funds are then redistributed to members for verified business expansion, giving every entrepreneur a real shot at growth without predatory interest rates.

**What's included:**
- Structured daily capital pooling and secure fund management
- Business training: financial management, bookkeeping, marketing, leadership
- One-on-one business advisory and mentorship
- Business continuity and risk-management planning
- Access to a network of fellow entrepreneurs for referrals, partnerships, and joint ventures
- Support in preparing investment proposals and feasibility studies

## SECTION 4 — Agribusiness, Farming Development & Export (Project L – Chapter B)

**Layout:** Two-column split (mirrored from Section 3 for rhythm) — left: image of farmers/cooperative work; right: text + bullet list. Below it, add a horizontal "resource allocation" bar chart or simple infographic showing the 30/70 split (30% reinvested in research, seeds, training & CSR; 70% toward tools, livestock, storage, and members' personal goals).

**Copy:**

**From the Field to the Global Market — Without the Middleman**

OB39 Ltd organizes farmers into cooperative units of roughly 15 households who work each other's land on a rotational basis, backed by scientifically tested seeds, fertilizers, and modern training. Harvests are aggregated and sold directly to high-value international buyers at fair prices — bypassing the multiple layers of middlemen that typically erode farmer income.

**What's included:**
- Farmer organization into collaborative 15-household units
- Certified seeds and fertilizers, matched to soil surveys
- Training in modern planting, harvesting, storage, and processing techniques
- Shared farm tools, livestock, and cold-storage/warehouse access
- Direct market linkage to international buyers meeting export standards
- Up to six capital/harvest cycles per year for continuous income

**How harvest proceeds are shared:**
- 30% reinvested into research, seeds, fertilizer, and training (5% of this toward community social responsibility projects)
- 70% directed to mutual growth — tools, ploughing bulls, storage infrastructure — and members' personal needs, prioritizing the least-advantaged members first

## SECTION 5 — Digital Marketplace & Market Access (Project OM)

**Layout:** Single-column, centered text block with a simple screenshot/mockup placeholder of the planned marketplace platform, plus a "Coming Soon" or "In Development" badge if not yet live.

**Copy:**

**Connecting Every Member to Bigger Markets**

Alongside physical trade exhibitions and partnerships, OB39 Ltd is building Project OM — a digital marketplace where member farmers and businesses can list produce and products, connect with verified local and international buyers, and track transactions transparently. It's designed to give every member — from a smallholder farmer to a growing small business — direct, uninterrupted access to markets that were previously out of reach.

## SECTION 6 — How It Works (Process Overview)

**Layout:** Horizontal 4-step timeline/stepper graphic (numbered circles connected by a line), responsive to a vertical stack on mobile.

**Copy:**

1. **Join a Unit** — Become part of a farming cooperative (15 households) or a business unit (60 members).
2. **Contribute & Access Resources** — Pool a share of profits or harvest, and gain access to training, tools, and inputs.
3. **Grow with Support** — Receive mentorship, financing, and shared resources tailored to your stage of growth.
4. **Reach the Market** — Sell through fair-price channels, export networks, or the OM digital marketplace.

## Notes for Implementation
- Keep each service section's bullet list scannable — 4–6 bullets max, no dense paragraphs.
- Use consistent iconography across Sections 2, 7, and 8 to visually tie the page together.
- Section 5 (Project OM) should be clearly marked as in-development if the marketplace isn't live yet, to keep the page accurate.
- Consider anchor-linking the Section 2 cards to Sections 3–5 so visitors can jump straight to the service they care about.
